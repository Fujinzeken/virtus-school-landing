1. Project Goal and Context
   The objective is to increase the conversion rate of lead flow on the virtusschool.uz website by improving the transition from visitor to application/tour. We aim to present the school's unique value proposition (Guarantee of entry into a QS Top-100 university + 100% tuition refund) in a clear, credible, and legally compliant manner.
   Context: VIS is a new international school opening for the 2026–2027 academic year. The existing Next.js, trilingual site is technically sound but suffers from:
   Invisibility of the main value proposition.
   Lack of trust-building elements.
   Fragmented conversion flow.
   Incomplete lead source (UTM) tracking.
   Success Criterion: Increased form submissions for tours/applications and precise UTM source tracking in AmoCRM.

2. General Principles
   Maintain & Extend: Build upon the existing Next.js codebase.
   Mobile-First: Prioritize mobile experience (primary traffic source).
   Trilingual Consistency: All content must be identical in Uzbek, Russian, and English.
   Single-Action Focus: Offer one clear primary action per screen.
   Transparency: All claims must be verified and accurate (see Appendix A).

4. Critical Tasks (P0)
   P0-1. Hero Block Redesign
   Action: Update H1 to focus on the guarantee (e.g., "We prepare your child for a QS Top-100 university — guaranteed").
   Requirements: Concise subheadline explaining the refund mechanism; single primary CTA ("Book a Tour"); optimized assets for LCP < 2.5s.
   P0-2. Strengthen Guarantee Block
   Action: Move to the top 1-2 screens; ensure text accuracy (correcting "grand" to "grant").
   Requirements: Clear 3-4 step mechanism explanation; professional design (navy/mint/orange palette).
   P0-3. IB Program Visibility
   Action: Add a "trust strip" near the hero highlighting IB authorization and program stages (PYP/MYP/DP).
   Requirements: Ensure all IB logo usage complies with official legal guidelines.
   P0-4. Unified CTA Flow
   Action: Standardize all CTAs to "Book a Tour" with consistent design (orange #FFAE52).
   Requirements: Implement sticky/floating mobile CTA.
   P0-5. Analytics & UTM Integration
   Action: Ensure all form submissions transmit UTM parameters (source, medium, campaign, content, term) to AmoCRM.
   Requirements: Set up GA4/Yandex Metrica events (views, submissions, clicks).
