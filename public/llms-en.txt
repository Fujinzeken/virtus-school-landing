# Virtus International School

> Virtus International School (VIS) is a private international school in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, for children aged 4 to 19. The school runs IB programmes (PYP for ages 4–10 and MYP for ages 11–15) and prepares students for admission to the world's top 100 universities. The campus is a modern 6-storey building of 7,000 m² with capacity for 700 students. The site is available in three languages: Uzbek, Russian and English.

Virtus International School positions itself as affordable premium international education in Tashkent. Its core purpose is preparing students for admission to leading world universities (Top-100) and supporting them fully through the entrance examinations. The school states a guarantee of admission to a Top-100 university on a grant basis, otherwise tuition is refunded in full.

Key facts:

- Student ages: 4–19; new intake from age 4 up to age 15
- Class size: 18–20 students
- Campus: 6-storey building, 7,000 m², up to 700 students at once
- Academic year: 10 months, from 2 September to 30 June
- IB programmes: PYP (ages 4–10) and MYP (ages 11–15)
- Educational tracks: Exact Sciences (mathematics, physics, IT), Natural Sciences (chemistry, biology, geography), Social Sciences and Humanities (history, law, economics, business management)
- Taught in depth across all tracks: mathematics and English
- Language of instruction and of entrance examinations: English
- Grants: 50 full (100%) grants; tuition discounts from 5% to 100% based on competitive entrance examinations; awarded per academic quarter, retention requires staying within the Top 5 of the grade and upholding IB disciplinary standards
- Family discounts: 5% for each additional child from the same family; 10% on annual payment
- Summer programme preparing children for the entrance examinations is available
